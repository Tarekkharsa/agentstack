# Distribution: how users arrive

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

"Ship to get evidence" is hollow without a path from publish to first user. For the named evidence target (01): what is the channel (launch post, registry listings, the eve ecosystem's users, direct outreach), what is the positioning one-liner, and what does the v0.18.0 release announcement lead with? v3 needs at least the channel decision; copy can follow later.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-08-02 by maintainer decision in a wayfinder grilling session.

**Channels, in priority order:**

1. **Show HN** — the primary launch moment. The audience is dense there, a CLI tool with a security story is HN-shaped, and it produces the fastest burst of real-install evidence for the real-usage trigger.
2. **CLI communities, same day** — Claude Code and Codex community spaces (Discord, Reddit, X): pre-qualified readers who all run at least one beachhead CLI.
3. **Skills-ecosystem wedge, slow burn** — presence in vercel-labs/skills discussions, awesome-MCP lists, and eve's ecosystem, positioned as the governed way to use what those registries distribute.

Direct outreach was considered and dropped as a named channel — it can happen informally without the strategy naming it.

**The lead: portability first.** The announcement opens with the carried-forward promise — *"Define your agent setup once. Use it across every coding CLI."* — with trust as the second beat: the reason it is dependable, not the hook. Wedge-channel posts may invert the emphasis locally; the launch itself does not hitch to a competitor's name.

Copy is execution, written after the study passes; the timing rule stands — launch happens only after the study result trigger fires and the three blockers are fixed.

### Amended 2026-08-02 (later the same day)

Channels and lead stand unchanged. The timing rule changes: launch follows the maintainer-happy declaration (and the study, when it then runs) — not a pre-release study pass.

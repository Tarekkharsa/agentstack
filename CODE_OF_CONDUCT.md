# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AgentStack welcomes people who participate in good faith. Be respectful,
specific, and constructive. Disagreement about code, product direction, or
security claims is expected; harassment and personal attacks are not.

Examples of acceptable participation include:

- criticizing an idea or implementation without attacking its author;
- giving enough context for another person to reproduce a problem;
- respecting privacy, credentials, and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- accepting a maintainer decision without continuing to disrupt the thread.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, deliberate
misrepresentation, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
release channels, and other spaces where someone represents AgentStack.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately by contacting the maintainer on GitHub,
[@Tarekkharsa](https://github.com/Tarekkharsa). For a security
vulnerability, use the separate private route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently ban participation.
The response will be proportional to the behavior and will protect the privacy
of reporters where possible. If a report concerns the maintainer, that same
GitHub route is still the only private one; the solo-maintainer limitation is
recorded in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) rather than hidden.
